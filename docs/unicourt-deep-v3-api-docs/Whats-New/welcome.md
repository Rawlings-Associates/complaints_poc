# Welcome to DEEP v3!

DEEP v3 is UniCourt's upgraded API platform for court records, case data, and entity resolution. Deliver the industry's most comprehensive litigation data and analytics directly into your applications, workflows, and data platforms.

## New API capabilities

- **Case Track** — Keeps a case up to date on a schedule you choose, without manual polling or one-off update calls. Case Track is a core way to stay up to date on docket activity: set the cadence (as frequently as hourly), and UniCourt refreshes for you.
- **Case Import** — Bring cases into your workspace from court sources so you can work with them through the rest of the DEEP API.
- **Document Text Search** — Search OCR'd text across hundreds of millions of downloaded documents for keyword hits inside specific filings.
- **Case Match** — Fuzzy-match your internal case records to the right UniCourt case, even when those records are incomplete or inconsistent.
- **Normalized Entity Match** — Apply the same fuzzy matching for attorneys and law firms, including mapping several of your internal records to a single UniCourt entity.
- **Case History** — Follow a change log across parties, counsel, judges, docket entries, hearings, case documents, tentative rulings, and related cases so you can see what changed and when.
- **Court Service Status Notifications** — Know when services like case updates and document orders go offline or come back for the courts you care about.

## Account usage management

Workspaces are shared between DEEP and DART. Cases tracked in one show up in the other, so activity and usage stay consistent no matter which platform your team is working from. Use the new usage APIs to view usage by account or by workspace. Flexible token management—including workspace-scoped tokens—controls access the way your team is organized.

## New entity normalization system

Our upgraded normalization engine delivers more robust attorney and law firm profiles, with far fewer duplicates and fragments, so the entity you match is the entity you meant. We’ve also improved how attorneys and law firms are structured in the API, providing clearer connections to cases and to each other, so you can more easily understand the role of an attorney or law firm.

## Coming soon

Tags (motion types and outcomes, case events), judgments, complaint summaries, and more. We're also continuing to deepen enrichment of docket and document data, with additional high-value additions to the case object planned over time.

## Upgrade within v3 on a schedule you control, on a timeline you know

Each GA release is identified by its release date. It uses calendar-based versioning, allowing developers to easily know exactly when a new version was released. Every GA release has a clear lifecycle, with published retirement dates to help you plan for seamless upgrades.
